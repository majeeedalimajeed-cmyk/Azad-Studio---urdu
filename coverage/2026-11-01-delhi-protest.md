# Delhi protest (1 Nov 2026) — DO NOT COVER controversial angles

Operator decision (29 Oct 2026): **no controversial news of the
protest will be covered** — not on YouTube, not on Facebook.

That means none of the following, in any post or video:
- Resignation / impeachment demands against any official
- "Vote theft" or similar allegations and slogans
- Disputed turnout claims by any side
- Accusations between parties about the protest

The 29 Oct protest-preparations update video was withdrawn before
posting and deleted for this reason.

If anything is ever carried from the event, it may only be plain,
non-controversial civic information (e.g. official traffic or public
safety advisories) — verified, factual, and with a matched photo.
When in doubt: skip it.
